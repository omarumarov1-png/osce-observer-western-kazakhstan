Polling Stations — Western Kazakhstan

An interactive map of polling station (UIK) locations across Aktobe and West Kazakhstan oblasts. Data is drawn from official akimat decrees on polling station boundaries; addresses have been geocoded and cross-checked against independent sources. Choose a region below to open its map.

Coordinates were generated by geocoding official addresses (OpenStreetMap Nominatim), cross-checked against 2GIS where coverage allowed. Some rural polling stations are pinned at the settlement level rather than the exact building — these are marked "≈ approximate" and should be verified on the ground before being relied on for fieldwork. Addresses, institution names, and administrative-division labels are kept in their original Russian/Kazakh, matching real-world signage and local GPS apps.
